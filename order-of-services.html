Sunday Order of Services

Every Sunday morning we gather in Tijeras to study Scripture across all ages, share coffee and conversation, worship together, and enjoy a church family meal.

Sunday at a Glance
9:30 AM Classes 10:25 AM Coffee 11:00 AM Worship 12:30 PM Potluck

9:30 AM

Sunday Bible Classes & Nursery Care

We offer engaging Bible classes for all ages beginning at 9:30 AM. A current room directory is posted in the church foyer. Nursery care is available during this hour for newborns through age 3 (contact Nursery Coordinator Lisa Bell via the Church Office at 505.281.4105).

Nursery (0-3) Preschool (3-5) 1st-2nd Grade 3rd-5th Grade Middle School (6-8) High School Adult Study 1 Adult Study 2
10:25 AM

Fellowship Time

Meet us on the church lawn (or foyer in winter weather) after morning Bible classes for coffee, cookies, and warm conversation before the Worship Service.

11:00 AM

Worship Service (In-Person & Livestream)

Join us in the Sanctuary at 11:00 AM for corporate worship, prayer, and biblical instruction. We love seeing families worship together; for the convenience of nursing mothers and parents with small children, the service is broadcast live into both the Nursery and the Youth Room.

12:30 PM

All-Church Potluck Lunch

Open to visitors and members alike, our potluck lunch is a joyful time to share a meal and build deeper friendships in the Fellowship Hall or outdoors on the lawn. Held every Sunday except the first Sunday of each month (our 1.1.12 one-on-one fellowship focus) and occasional announced exceptions.
