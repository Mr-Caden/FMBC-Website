Over 60 Years of Faithfulness

Since faithful believers first began praying and laboring in this corner of the Sandia Mountains in the 1940s, God has sustained Forest Meadow as a Scripture-anchored home for generations of East Mountains families.

1940s - 1982

Planted in Prayer & Perseverance

Forest Meadow was officially incorporated in 1982 as a small country church tucked into a corner of the Sandia Mountains. Yet the story of the church truly began decades earlier with the vision and prayers of faithful saints moving in this area since the 1940s. Many hands from different churches labored diligently on this land for over four decades, each contributing a chapter to the work the Lord was building—and the church endured faithfully even through dry seasons and difficult days.

1999 - 2020

Twenty Years of Shepherding

Beginning in 1999, Forest Meadow was shepherded for twenty years by Pastor Tom Lambert. Together with his wife Tammy, their family faithfully led and served the church body, orchestrated outreach to the local East Mountains community, and discipled future leadership.

2021 - Today

Growing & Bearing Fruit

In 2021, Mike Hernandez accepted the role of Lead Pastor. Today he, his wife Chelsea, and their family continue to love and serve the community they have labored alongside for many years. By the grace of God, Forest Meadow continues to grow and bear fruit.

Why We Exist

Our Mission

Here at Forest Meadow, our mission is to encourage, instruct, and equip the body of Christ, to make disciples, and to see the East Mountains renewed by the Gospel.

We are a broken but forgiven people who seek to build the Kingdom, to love our neighbors, and to lay stepping stones for eternity, so that those who come behind us might know the Lord, walk in His ways, and have hope as they journey on the narrow way toward our true Home.

What We Confess

Our Beliefs

We believe in one God, the Creator of all things. We firmly believe the Bible is His inerrant Word. We confess that Jesus Christ is the Son of God, that He is the only Savior, and that salvation is a free gift of grace through His blood. By Jesus' death and resurrection we receive the forgiveness of sins and the fellowship of the Holy Spirit. Together with all of God's people worldwide, we await the return of Christ and the resurrection of the dead.

Theologically, FMBC subscribes to the Baptist Faith and Message. We partner with the Southern Baptist Convention of New Mexico and other like-minded churches, while remaining an independent body unified and led by the Holy Spirit. Our elders, deacons, and staff form a leadership team that works together to serve our congregation.
