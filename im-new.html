What to Expect on Sunday

Sunday Bible classes begin at 9:30 AM. This is followed at 10:25 AM by a time of fellowship, coffee, and conversation. Our Worship Service begins at 11:00 AM in the Sanctuary.

We offer an array of Bible classes for children, youth, and adults—some meeting on Sunday mornings at 9:30 AM and others throughout the week (Tuesday Ladies & Men's studies, Wednesday Pathfinders & Themelios Youth, Kaleo College, and Parents Bible Study). For details, please contact Bible Study Coordinator Isaac Parks at parks@forestmeadow.church.

Nursery care is available for children 0-3 years old from 9:30-10:30 AM during our Sunday morning Bible classes, alongside classes for older children, teens, and adults. If this is your first time joining us, feel free to arrive 5-10 minutes early to meet teachers and locate classrooms. Please note: there is no nursery care or children's church during the 11:00 AM worship service, as we encourage families to worship and learn together. Because we know transitions can be tough for little ones, we always stream the service live into both the Youth Room and Nursery for nursing mothers and restless children.

FMBC is a mountain church located in a rural area, and our members come from all walks of life. You will see a wide range of attire from slacks and dress shirts to jeans and work boots. Please wear whatever is comfortable and appropriate for you and the mountain weather.

Our potluck lunch is open to everyone—visitors and members alike! We host lunch nearly every Sunday at 12:30 PM immediately following worship (outdoors on the lawn or in the Fellowship Hall depending on weather), except on the first Sunday of the month when our focus is 1.1.12: a ministry of 1-on-1 relationship building once a month, twelve times a year. Please join us—we would love to share a meal and get to know you.
