Caring for Our Neighbors

Benevolence Ministry

Forest Meadow's Benevolence Fund exists to extend the tangible compassion of Christ to members of our church family and neighbors throughout the East Mountains who are walking through unexpected financial hardship, medical expenses, utility or heating needs, or family emergencies.

Every dollar designated for Benevolence is stewarded prayerfully and confidentially by our Deacons and church leadership to meet real, practical needs with dignity and Gospel care.

How to Support or Request Care

Giving to or Reaching Benevolence

  • Designate a Gift: Select the Benevolence Fund when giving online, or write "Benevolence" in the memo line of any in-person or mailed check.
  • Confidential Assistance: If you or a neighbor in the East Mountains are experiencing a season of hardship, please reach out confidentially to our Church Office at 505.281.4105 or info@forestmeadow.church.
  • Questions: Contact Deacon & Treasurer Bill Miller or our Elders through the church office.
